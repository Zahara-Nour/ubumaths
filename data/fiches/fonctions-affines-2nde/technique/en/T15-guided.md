Let ~f~ and ~g~ be the affine functions defined on $\mathbb{R}$ by ~f(x)=3x-1~ and ~g(x)=-x+7~. The lines $\mathcal{D}_f$ and $\mathcal{D}_g$ representing them are drawn below.

```courbe
x: -2 ; 5
y: -4 ; 10
grille: 1 ; 1
f(x) = 3*x-1   bleu   nom=\mathcal{D}_f
g(x) = -x+7   orange   nom=\mathcal{D}_g
taille: moyenne
description: Two straight lines on a grid with squares of side 1, with x from -2 to 5 and y from -4 to 10: the line Df goes up, the line Dg goes down, and they meet at one point.
```

1. Read the coordinates of the point common to $\mathcal{D}_f$ and $\mathcal{D}_g$.
2. Find this point again by calculation, by solving the equation ~f(x)=g(x)~.
3. We are looking for the real numbers ~x~ such that ~f(x)>g(x)~.
   a. Solve this inequality graphically.
   b. Solve it by calculation.
