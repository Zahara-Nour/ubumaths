1. Let ~f~ be the function defined on $\mathbb{R}$ by ~f(x)=-3x+2~. Without working out the images, compare:
   a. $f(\sqrt{2})$ and ~f(1.4)~;
   b. ~f(-\pi)~ and ~f(-3)~.

2. Let ~g~ be the function defined on $\mathbb{R}$ by ~g(x)=x/4+1~. Without working out the images, compare ~g(0.001)~ and ~g(0.01)~.

3. ~h~ is an affine function such that ~h(2)=5~ and ~h(7)=1~. Is it increasing, decreasing or constant? Justify your answer.
