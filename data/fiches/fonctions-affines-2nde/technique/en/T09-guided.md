Let ~f~ be the affine function defined on $\mathbb{R}$ by ~f(x)=-2x+5~, and $\mathcal{D}_f$ the line representing it.

1. Do the following points lie on $\mathcal{D}_f$? Justify with a calculation.

   :colonnes 2
   a. $A(1\,;3)$
   b. $B(-2\,;1)$
   c. $C\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\,;2\right)$
   d. $D(10\,;-14)$

2. The point $E(-4\,;y)$ lies on $\mathcal{D}_f$. Work out ~y~.
3. The point $F(x\,;0)$ lies on $\mathcal{D}_f$. Work out ~x~.
