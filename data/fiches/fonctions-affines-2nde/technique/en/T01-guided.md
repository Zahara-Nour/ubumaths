Consider the following functions. They are defined on $\mathbb{R}$, except ~f_6~, which is defined for every non-zero real number ~x~.

:colonnes 2
- ~f_1(x)=3x-5~
- ~f_2(x)=-2x~
- ~f_3(x)=x^2+1~
- ~f_4(x)=7~
- ~f_5(x)={x-4}/3~
- ~f_6(x)=1/x+2~
- ~f_7(x)=5-x~
- ~f_8(x)=sqrt(2)x~

1. For each function, say whether it is linear, affine, or neither.
2. For each affine function, write its expression in the form ~mx+p~, then give its gradient ~m~ and its y-intercept ~p~.
3. Is a linear function an affine function? Justify your answer.
