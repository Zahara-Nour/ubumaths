When we know ~m~ and one image ~f(a)=b~, we find ~p~ by substituting into ~f(a)=ma+p~, then we solve the equation whose unknown is ~p~.

1. ~f(2)=1~ gives ~3*2+p=1~, that is ~6+p=1~, so ~p=-5~.

   ~f(x)=3x-5~.

2. ~f(4)=0~ gives ~-1/2*4+p=0~, that is ~-2+p=0~, so ~p=2~.

   ~f(x)=-1/2x+2~.

3. The point $A(-1\,;2)$ lies on $\mathcal{D}_f$, so ~f(-1)=2~. This gives ~4*(-1)+p=2~, that is ~-4+p=2~, so ~p=6~.

   ~f(x)=4x+6~.

4. ~g~ is linear: ~g(x)=mx~ (here ~p=0~). ~g(5)=-15~ gives ~5m=-15~, so ~m=-3~.

   ~g(x)=-3x~.
