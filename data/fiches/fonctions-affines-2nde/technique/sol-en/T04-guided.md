When ~x~ is given, we work out its image: we replace ~x~ with its value in ~2x-3~. When the image ~k~ is given, we look for its preimage: we solve the equation ~f(x)=k~.

1. ~f(-2)=2*(-2)-3=-7~, ~f(-1)=2*(-1)-3=-5~, ~f(0)=-3~, ~f(1/2)=2*1/2-3=-2~ and ~f(3)=2*3-3=3~.

   | $x$ | $-2$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $\dfrac{1}{2}$ | $3$ |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | $f(x)$ | $-7$ | $-5$ | $-3$ | $-2$ | $3$ |

2. We solve ~2x-3=7~: ~2x=10~, so ~x=5~.

   We solve ~2x-3=0~: ~2x=3~, so ~x=3/2~.

   | $x$ | $5$ | $\dfrac{3}{2}$ |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | $f(x)$ | $7$ | $0$ |
