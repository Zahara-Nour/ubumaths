A function ~f(x)=mx+p~ is increasing if ~m>0~; ~m~ is the increase in ~f(x)~ when ~x~ increases by ~1~. To go from a value of ~f(x)~ back to ~x~, solve the equation ~f(x)=k~.

1.
   a. ~f(x)=1.8x+32~ has the form ~mx+p~ with ~m=1.8~ and ~p=32~: ~f~ is affine.
   b. ~m=1.8>0~: ~f~ is increasing on $\mathbb{R}$. The higher a temperature is in degrees Celsius, the higher it is in degrees Fahrenheit: both scales put temperatures in the same order.
   c. When the temperature rises by 1 °C, it rises by $1.8$ °F.
2.
   a. ~f(0)=1.8*0+32=32~. Water freezes at ~32~ °F.
   b. ~f(100)=1.8*100+32=180+32=212~. Water boils at ~212~ °F.
3. We look for ~x~ such that ~f(x)=98.6~:

   ~~1.8x+32=98.6~~

   ~~1.8x=66.6~~

   ~~x=66.6/1.8=37~~

   $98.6$ °F is 37 °C, the normal temperature of the human body.

4. Solve ~f(x)=x~:

   ~~1.8x+32=x~~

   ~~0.8x=-32~~

   ~~x=-32/0.8=-40~~

   Check: ~f(-40)=-72+32=-40~. The temperature −40 °C is also −40 °F: it is the only temperature written with the same number on both scales.
