The price starts from a fixed value (the pick-up fee) and increases by the same amount for each kilometre: it is modelled by an affine function ~f(x)=mx+p~.

1. The price is the pick-up fee, plus €$1.50$ per kilometre:

   ~~f(x)=1.5x+4~~

   ~f~ is affine, with ~m=1.5~ and ~p=4~. The y-intercept ~p=4~ is the pick-up fee, paid before the taxi moves; the gradient ~m=1.5~ is the price of one kilometre.

2. ~f(12)=1.5*12+4=22~: a ride of ~12~ km costs €$22$.

   ~f(x)=31~ gives ~1.5x=27~, so ~x=18~: the ride costing €$31$ was ~18~ km long.

3. Solve ~f(x)<=40~: ~1.5x<=36~, so ~x<=24~ (dividing by ~1.5>0~ does not change the sign). The customer can travel **at most ~24~ km**.
