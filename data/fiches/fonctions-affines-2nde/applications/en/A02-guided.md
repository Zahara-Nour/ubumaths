In France, temperatures are measured in degrees Celsius (°C); in the United States, in degrees Fahrenheit (°F). To convert degrees Celsius into degrees Fahrenheit, you multiply by $1.8$, then add ~32~.

Let ~f~ be the function that maps a temperature ~x~ in degrees Celsius to the same temperature ~f(x)~ in degrees Fahrenheit. So ~f(x)=1.8x+32~.

1. Studying the function ~f~.
   a. Explain why ~f~ is an affine function. Give its gradient ~m~ and its y-intercept ~p~.
   b. Is ~f~ increasing or decreasing? Interpret this result for temperatures.
   c. What does the gradient ~m~ mean for temperatures?
2. Converting into degrees Fahrenheit.
   a. Water freezes at 0 °C. What is this temperature in degrees Fahrenheit?
   b. Water boils at 100 °C. What is this temperature in degrees Fahrenheit?
3. An American doctor measures a temperature of $98.6$ °F. What is this temperature in degrees Celsius?
4. Is there a temperature that is written with the same number on both scales? To find out, solve the equation ~f(x)=x~.
