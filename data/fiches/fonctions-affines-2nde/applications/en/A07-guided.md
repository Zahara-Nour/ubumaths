A quantity ~y~ is **proportional** to a quantity ~x~ when ~y=mx~: ~y~ is then a linear function of ~x~. A linear function is a special affine function, whose y-intercept is zero.

1. For each situation, express ~y~ in terms of ~x~, then say whether ~y~ is proportional to ~x~, whether ~y~ is an affine function of ~x~ that is not linear, or neither.
   a. ~y~ is the perimeter, in cm, of a square with side ~x~ cm.
   b. ~y~ is the area, in cm², of a square with side ~x~ cm.
   c. An online shop charges €$5$ delivery per order, plus €$2$ per item. ~y~ is the price, in euros, of an order of ~x~ items.
2. In each of the following tables, is ~y~ an affine function of ~x~? To find out, work out the rate of change $\dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$ between each column and the next. When ~y~ is an affine function of ~x~, give its expression.
   a. First table:

      | $x$ | $1$ | $2$ | $4$ | $7$ |
      |:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
      | $y$ | $5$ | $8$ | $14$ | $23$ |

   b. Second table:

      | $x$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
      |:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
      | $y$ | $1$ | $2$ | $4$ | $8$ |
