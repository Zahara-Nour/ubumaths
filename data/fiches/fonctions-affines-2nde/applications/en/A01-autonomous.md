In a town, the price of a taxi ride is made up of a pick-up fee of €$4$, plus €$1.50$ for each kilometre travelled. Let ~f(x)~ be the price, in euros, of a ride of ~x~ kilometres.

![A taxi and its taximeter: pick-up fee €4, price per kilometre €1.50](https://cnevnzsvixxpnurautls.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/exercise-images/97c1d5e4-5fa0-44ce-be83-ed5467f3a424/affine-taxi-en.png){size=large align=center}

1. Express ~f(x)~ in terms of ~x~. Explain why ~f~ is affine and interpret its gradient and its y-intercept.
2. What is the price of a ride of ~12~ km? What distance was travelled for a ride costing €$31$?
3. A customer has €$40$. What is the greatest distance the customer can travel?
