A car sets off with a full tank of ~60~ litres of fuel. It uses fuel steadily, at a rate of ~6~ litres per ~100~ km.

Let ~f(x)~ be the volume of fuel, in litres, left in the tank after ~x~ kilometres.

![A fuel gauge marked from 0 to 60 litres, with a red reserve zone from 0 to 15 litres; the needle shows a full tank](https://cnevnzsvixxpnurautls.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/exercise-images/97c1d5e4-5fa0-44ce-be83-ed5467f3a424/affine-reservoir-en.png){size=large align=center}

1. Building the model.
   a. What volume of fuel does the car use for each kilometre travelled?
   b. Explain why ~f(x)=60-0.06x~.
   c. Explain why ~f~ is an affine function. Give its gradient ~m~ and its y-intercept ~p~.
2. Increasing or decreasing.
   a. Is ~f~ increasing or decreasing? Could you have predicted it?
   b. Interpret the gradient ~m~ in this situation.
3. After how many kilometres does the car run out of fuel? Deduce the interval on which the function ~f~ models the situation.
4. The low-fuel warning light comes on when ~15~ litres are left in the tank. After how many kilometres does it come on?
