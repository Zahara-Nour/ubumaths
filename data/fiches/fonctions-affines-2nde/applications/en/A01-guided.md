In a town, the price of a taxi ride is made up of a pick-up fee of €$4$, plus €$1.50$ for each kilometre travelled.

Let ~f(x)~ be the price, in euros, of a ride of ~x~ kilometres, for $x\geqslant 0$.

![A taxi and its taximeter: pick-up fee €4, price per kilometre €1.50](https://cnevnzsvixxpnurautls.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/exercise-images/97c1d5e4-5fa0-44ce-be83-ed5467f3a424/affine-taxi-en.png){size=large align=center}

1. Building the model.
   a. Work out the price of a ride of ~1~ km, then of a ride of ~2~ km.
   b. Express ~f(x)~ in terms of ~x~.
   c. Explain why ~f~ is an affine function. Give its gradient ~m~ and its y-intercept ~p~.
   d. What do ~m~ and ~p~ mean for the customer?
2. Using the model.
   a. Work out the price of a ride of ~12~ km.
   b. A ride cost €$31$. What distance did the taxi travel?
   c. A customer has €$40$. Write an inequality that describes the situation, then solve it. What is the greatest distance the customer can travel?
