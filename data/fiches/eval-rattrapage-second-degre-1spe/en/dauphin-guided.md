During a show, a dolphin leaps out of the water and dives back in **6 m** further on. Let ~x~ be the horizontal distance, in metres, between the dolphin and the point where it left the water, and ~h(x)~ its height above the water, in metres. ~h~ is modelled by a quadratic function. At **1 m** from where it left the water, the dolphin is **2.5 m** above the water.

![dolphin](https://cnevnzsvixxpnurautls.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/exercise-images/97c1d5e4-5fa0-44ce-be83-ed5467f3a424/rattrapage-dauphin-en.png){size=large align=center}

1. Explain why ~h(x)~ can be written $h(x)=a\,x(x-6)$, then show that $a=-\dfrac{1}{2}$.
2. Show that, for every real number ~x~, $h(x)=-0.5x^2+3x$, then that $h(x)=-0.5(x-3)^2+4.5$.
3. What is the maximum height reached by the dolphin? How far from where it left the water?
4. The trainer holds a hoop **4 m** above the water. At which distances from the exit point can the hoop be placed so that the dolphin jumps through it?
5. A photographer can only capture the dolphin when it is **at least 2.5 m** above the water. Over what horizontal distance can the dolphin be photographed?
