A town square is shaped like a square ~ABCD~ with sides of **10 m**. Points ~M~ on $[AB]$, ~N~ on $[BC]$, ~P~ on $[CD]$ and ~Q~ on $[DA]$ are placed so that $AM=BN=CP=DQ=x$, in metres. The central part ~MNPQ~ is paved; the four triangles in the corners are planted with flowers.

![flower beds](https://cnevnzsvixxpnurautls.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/exercise-images/97c1d5e4-5fa0-44ce-be83-ed5467f3a424/rattrapage-massif.png){size=large align=center}

1. Which values can ~x~ take?
2. Express the area of triangle ~AMQ~ in terms of ~x~, then show that the paved area is $S(x)=2x^2-20x+100$.
3. Paving is expensive: for which value of ~x~ is the paved area as small as possible? What is this area?
4. The town wants at least $42\ \text{m}^2$ of flowers. Which values of ~x~ can it choose?
