A craftswoman makes and sells candles; she can produce **at most 50** a day. The cost of making ~x~ candles, in euros, is
$$C(x)=0.5x^2+6x+160$$
Each candle sells for **€30**, and every candle made is sold. Given: $24^2=576$.

1. Express the revenue ~R(x)~ in terms of ~x~, then show that the profit is $B(x)=-0.5x^2+24x-160$.
2. Check that, for every real number ~x~, $B(x)=-0.5(x-8)(x-40)$.
3. For which numbers of candles does the craftswoman make a profit? Justify with a sign table.
4. How many candles should she make and sell to maximise her profit? What is this profit?
