1. The revenue is the selling price times the number of candles sold: $R(x)=30x$, with $0\leqslant x\leqslant50$.
   The profit is revenue minus cost: $B(x)=30x-\left(0.5x^2+6x+160\right)=-0.5x^2+24x-160$.
2. $-0.5(x-8)(x-40)=-0.5\left(x^2-40x-8x+320\right)=-0.5\left(x^2-48x+320\right)=-0.5x^2+24x-160=B(x)$.
3. The roots of ~B~ are ~8~ and ~40~, and the coefficient ~-0.5~ is negative: ~B(x)~ has the sign of ~-0.5~ (negative) outside the roots, and is positive between them.

   ```variation
   variable: x
   domain: 0, 8, 40, 50

   sign: B(x)
     0,8: -
     8: z
     8,40: +
     40: z
     40,50: -
   ```

   The craftswoman makes a profit when $B(x)>0$, that is for **more than 8 and fewer than 40 candles** (from 9 to 39 candles a day).
4. The coefficient ~-0.5~ is negative: ~B~ has a maximum at $\alpha=-\dfrac{24}{2\times(-0.5)}=24$, which does lie in $[0~;~50]$ (it is also halfway between the roots 8 and 40).
   $B(24)=-0.5\times576+24\times24-160=-288+576-160=128$.
   The profit is maximal for **24 candles**; it is then **€128**.
