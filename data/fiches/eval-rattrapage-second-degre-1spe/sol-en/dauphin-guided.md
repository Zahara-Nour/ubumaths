1. The dolphin is at water level at ~x=0~ (it leaves the water) and at ~x=6~ (it dives back in): ~0~ and ~6~ are the roots of ~h~. A quadratic function with roots ~0~ and ~6~ has the factorised form $h(x)=a(x-0)(x-6)=a\,x(x-6)$.
   At 1 m from the exit point, the dolphin is 2.5 m high: $h(1)=a\times1\times(1-6)=-5a=2.5$, so $a=-\dfrac{2.5}{5}=-\dfrac{1}{2}$.
2. $h(x)=-\dfrac{1}{2}x(x-6)=-0.5x^2+3x$.
   And $-0.5(x-3)^2+4.5=-0.5\left(x^2-6x+9\right)+4.5=-0.5x^2+3x-4.5+4.5=-0.5x^2+3x=h(x)$.
3. In the vertex form $h(x)=-0.5(x-3)^2+4.5$, the coefficient ~-0.5~ is negative: ~h~ has a maximum, reached at the vertex $S(3~;~4.5)$. The dolphin reaches **4.5 m**, **3 m** from the exit point (halfway through the jump, by symmetry).
4. $h(x)=4\iff-0.5(x-3)^2+4.5=4\iff(x-3)^2=1\iff x-3=-1$ or $x-3=1\iff x=2$ or $x=4$. Both solutions lie in $[0~;~6]$ and make sense: the dolphin is 4 m high once on the way up and once on the way down. The trainer can place the hoop **2 m or 4 m** from the exit point.
5. $h(x)\geqslant2.5\iff-0.5(x-3)^2+4.5\geqslant2.5\iff(x-3)^2\leqslant4\iff-2\leqslant x-3\leqslant2\iff1\leqslant x\leqslant5$. The dolphin is at least 2.5 m high between 1 m and 5 m from the exit point: the photographer has **4 m** of trajectory.
