1. ~M~ lies on $[AB]$, so $0\leqslant x\leqslant10$. For the four flower triangles to exist, we need ~x>0~ and ~10-x>0~: we take $0<x<10$.
2. Triangle ~AMQ~ is right-angled at ~A~, with $AM=x$ and $AQ=AD-DQ=10-x$. Its area is $\dfrac{x(10-x)}{2}$.
   The four corner triangles have the same area (same sides around the right angle). The paved area is therefore
   $$\begin{align*} S(x) &= 10^2-4\times\dfrac{x(10-x)}{2}=100-2x(10-x) \\ &= 100-20x+2x^2=2x^2-20x+100 \end{align*}$$
3. The coefficient of ~x^2~ is ~2>0~: ~S~ has a **minimum** at $\alpha=-\dfrac{-20}{2\times2}=5$, which does lie in $]0~;~10[$. $S(5)=2\times25-100+100=50$. The paved area is smallest for **~x=5~ m**: it is then **$50\ \text{m}^2$** (points ~M~, ~N~, ~P~, ~Q~ are the midpoints of the sides).
4. The flower area is $F(x)=100-S(x)=-2x^2+20x$.
   $F(x)\geqslant42\iff-2x^2+20x-42\geqslant0\iff x^2-10x+21\leqslant0$ (dividing by ~-2<0~ reverses the inequality).
   $\Delta=(-10)^2-4\times1\times21=100-84=16$, roots $\dfrac{10-4}{2}=3$ and $\dfrac{10+4}{2}=7$.
   The quadratic $x^2-10x+21$ (leading coefficient ~1>0~) is negative or zero between its roots: $3\leqslant x\leqslant7$, which does satisfy $0<x<10$. The town can choose **~x~ between 3 m and 7 m**.
