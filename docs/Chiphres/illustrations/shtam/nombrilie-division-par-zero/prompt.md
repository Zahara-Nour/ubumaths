# Dessin du Shtam — nombrilie-division-par-zero

```text
Draw a newspaper illustration for a satirical article: a schoolboy has just divided by zero on his calculator and the whole town is being evacuated as a precaution.

Scene: in the foreground on the left, a small schoolboy in a school smock and a beret looks sheepish and slightly proud, holding out a small pocket calculator at arm's length; from its screen escapes a strange swirling spiral of smoke that twists upward into the sky (no symbols on the screen). Two town guards in helmets are carefully wrapping a second identical calculator in a box tied with string and sealed with red wax. In the center right, the queen from the attached character image (Mère Ubu, same face, same crimson bustle dress, same style) stands calm and authoritative in front of a large bronze spiral statue, pointing the way out with her closed fan. Behind her, townspeople leave the town in an orderly line, carrying bundles, birdcages and a grandfather clock, glancing worriedly back at the spiral in the sky.

Style: 1896 Montmartre lithographic print in the manner of Théophile-Alexandre Steinlen, as a press drawing printed in an old newspaper: bold thick dark contours, flat color areas, no gradients, no 3D, flat shadow shapes only. Funny, never scary, for students aged 11 to 17.

Colors: use only the colors of the attached palette image (muted printing inks on cream paper): navy ink for the outlines, deep crimson for Mère Ubu's dress and the wax seal, marigold yellow and brown for the bronze statue and her tiara, emerald green for the guards' uniforms, grey-blue for the swirling smoke, cream parchment for the background.

Composition: horizontal 3:2 landscape format, the schoolboy and the guards on the left, Mère Ubu and the evacuation on the right, the spiral rising between them, plain cream paper background around the scene. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signature.
```
