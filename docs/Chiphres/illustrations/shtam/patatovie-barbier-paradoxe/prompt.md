Draw a newspaper illustration for a satirical article: a barber, standing in front of his own mirror, cannot decide whether he is allowed to shave himself.

Scene: an 1900s barbershop. Style: 1896 Montmartre lithographic print in the manner of Théophile-Alexandre Steinlen, as a press drawing printed in an old newspaper: bold thick dark contours, flat color areas, no gradients, no 3D, flat shadow shapes only. Funny, never scary, for students aged 11 to 17.

Colors: use only the colors of the attached palette image (muted printing inks on cream paper): navy ink for the outlines, deep crimson and white for the striped barber pole and the barber's waistcoat, marigold yellow for the brass fittings and the mirror frame, emerald green for the leather barber chair, cream parchment for the background.

Composition: horizontal 3:2 landscape format, the barber and his reflection facing each other in the center. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signature.
