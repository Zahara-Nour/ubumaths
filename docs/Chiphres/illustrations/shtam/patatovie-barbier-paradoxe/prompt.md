# Dessin du Shtam — patatovie-barbier-paradoxe

Pièces jointes (nouvelle conversation Nano Banana) : `nuancier-imprimerie-reference.png`.

```text
Draw a newspaper illustration for a satirical article: a barber, standing in front of his own mirror, cannot decide whether he is allowed to shave himself.

Scene: an 1900s barbershop. The barber, a thin, proud man with slicked hair and a big moustache, a white apron over a crimson waistcoat, stands in front of a large gilded mirror, a straight razor raised halfway in one hand, a shaving brush in the other, frozen in deep perplexity, eyebrows twisted. Only one of his cheeks is shaved and smooth; the other is covered with white shaving foam. His reflection in the mirror makes exactly the same puzzled face. Around him: an empty leather barber chair, a striped barber pole, shelves of bottles and towels, a few waiting customers with long beards reading newspapers with blank pages.

Style: 1896 Montmartre lithographic print in the manner of Théophile-Alexandre Steinlen, as a press drawing printed in an old newspaper: bold thick dark contours, flat color areas, no gradients, no 3D, flat shadow shapes only. Funny, never scary, for students aged 11 to 17.

Colors: use only the colors of the attached palette image (muted printing inks on cream paper): navy ink for the outlines, deep crimson and white for the striped barber pole and the barber's waistcoat, marigold yellow for the brass fittings and the mirror frame, emerald green for the leather barber chair, cream parchment for the background.

Composition: horizontal 3:2 landscape format, the barber and his reflection facing each other in the center. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signature.
```
