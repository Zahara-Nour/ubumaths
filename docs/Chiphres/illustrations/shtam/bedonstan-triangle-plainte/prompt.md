# Dessin du Shtam — bedonstan-triangle-plainte

Pièces jointes (nouvelle conversation Nano Banana) : `nuancier-imprimerie-reference.png`, `achras.jpg`.

```text
Draw a newspaper illustration for a satirical article: in a courtroom, a right triangle files a complaint because it was forced to have a hypotenuse it did not choose.

Scene: in the center, at the bar of a solemn 1890s courtroom, stands a large right triangle drawn as a character: a flat golden triangle with a clear square right-angle mark in its corner, small tick marks along its sides (three short ticks on the short side, four on the middle side, five on the long slanted side), a small indignant face, thin arms and legs; it waves a sheet of paper in protest, its right-angle corner trembling (little shake lines). Beside it on the left, the old scholar from the attached character image (Professeur Achras, same face, same green gown, same style) bends over the triangle, sorrowful and tender, one hand on its corner, like a farmer consoling his calf. Behind a high wooden bench, an old judge in a powdered wig yawns. On the right, through a tall window, a small group of irregular triangles of various shapes demonstrate in the street, holding up blank placards.

Style: 1896 Montmartre lithographic print in the manner of Théophile-Alexandre Steinlen, as a press drawing printed in an old newspaper: bold thick dark contours, flat color areas, no gradients, no 3D, flat shadow shapes only. Funny, never scary, for students aged 11 to 17.

Colors: use only the colors of the attached palette image (muted printing inks on cream paper): navy ink for the outlines, marigold yellow for the plaintiff triangle, emerald green for Achras's gown, deep crimson for the judge's robe and the drapes, brown for the wooden bench, cream parchment for the background.

Composition: horizontal 3:2 landscape format, Achras and the triangle in the center left, the judge in the background, the protesting triangles at the window on the right, plain cream paper background around the scene. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signature, blank placards.
```
