# Dessin du Shtam — yoyolande-hotel-de-l-infini

Pièces jointes (nouvelle conversation Nano Banana) : `nuancier-imprimerie-reference.png`.

```text
Draw a newspaper illustration for a satirical article: in a grand hotel with endlessly many rooms, every guest moves one room along so that a late traveler can have room one.

Scene: a long hotel corridor seen in strong perspective, with a door every few steps, going on and on until it disappears in a tiny point far away. In front of every door, a sleepy guest in nightgown and nightcap, carrying a suitcase, steps out and walks into the next door along the corridor, all doing the same gesture in a long repeating line. In the foreground, the concierge, a dignified man in a crimson uniform with gold buttons and a cap, calls the order through a brass speaking trumpet; next to him, a soaking wet traveler with an umbrella and a carpet bag smiles with relief in front of the first, now empty, open door.

Style: 1896 Montmartre lithographic print in the manner of Théophile-Alexandre Steinlen, as a press drawing printed in an old newspaper: bold thick dark contours, flat color areas, no gradients, no 3D, flat shadow shapes only. Funny, never scary, for students aged 11 to 17.

Colors: use only the colors of the attached palette image (muted printing inks on cream paper): navy ink for the outlines, deep crimson for the corridor carpet and the concierge's uniform, marigold yellow for the lamps and the brass room numbers plates (left blank), emerald green for the wallpaper, cream parchment for the background.

Composition: horizontal 3:2 landscape format, the concierge and the traveler in the foreground on the left, the corridor vanishing to the right. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signature.
```
