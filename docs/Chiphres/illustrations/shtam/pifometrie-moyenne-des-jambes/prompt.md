# Dessin du Shtam — pifometrie-moyenne-des-jambes

Pièces jointes (nouvelle conversation Nano Banana) : `nuancier-imprimerie-reference.png`, `capitaine-bordure.jpg`.

```text
Draw a newspaper illustration for a satirical article: on a town square, a stiff military captain has started counting, one by one, the legs of every inhabitant of the kingdom, to check that the average citizen really has fewer than two legs.

Scene: in the foreground on the left, the captain from the attached character image (Capitaine Bordure, same face, same uniform, same style) stands rigidly at attention, chin up, very serious, pointing a long wooden baton at the knee of the first person in line, as if counting it. In front of him, an endless queue of ordinary townspeople (peasants, a baker, a grandmother, a child), each with exactly two legs, all marching in step with one knee raised high at the same time; the queue zigzags across the square and disappears far away into the hills, very long. Behind them, a hosiery shop window displays socks hanging strictly in bundles of three, tied with ribbons. A bored townsman at the front of the queue looks down at his own two legs, puzzled.

Style: 1896 Montmartre lithographic print in the manner of Théophile-Alexandre Steinlen, as a press drawing printed in an old newspaper: bold thick dark contours, flat color areas, no gradients, no 3D, flat shadow shapes only. Funny, never scary, for students aged 11 to 17.

Colors: use only the colors of the attached palette image (muted printing inks on cream paper): navy ink for the outlines, emerald green for the captain's jacket, marigold yellow for its braid and epaulettes, deep crimson for his cap and sash (as on the attached character image), emerald green and brown for the townspeople's clothes and the shop front, marigold yellow and crimson for the bundled socks, cream parchment for the background.

Composition: horizontal 3:2 landscape format, the captain and the start of the queue on the left, the queue winding away to the right into the distance, plain cream paper background around the scene. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signature.
```
