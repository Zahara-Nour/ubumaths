# Dessin du Shtam — czar-alexis-pi-arrondi

Pièces jointes (nouvelle conversation Nano Banana) : `nuancier-imprimerie-reference.png`, `czar-alexis.jpg`.

```text
Draw a newspaper illustration for a satirical article: in the snowy courtyard of the Winter Palace, the Czar discovers that everything round has become slightly too short since he ordered pi to be rounded down to save money.

Scene: on the right, the emperor from the attached character image (Czar Alexis, same face, same uniform, same style) throws his arms up in outrage, mouth open in an exclamation. In the center, an imperial carriage stuck in the snow: each of its wheels has a rim that does not close, leaving a visible gap in the circle, so the carriage limps and tilts; the coachman scratches his head. On the left, through an open palace window, a round banquet table whose round tablecloth is visibly too small, leaving the edges of the table bare, and a garden fence around a round flower bed that stops just short of closing, leaving a gap. Snowflakes, onion-domed towers in the background.

Style: 1896 Montmartre lithographic print in the manner of Théophile-Alexandre Steinlen, as a press drawing printed in an old newspaper: bold thick dark contours, flat color areas, no gradients, no 3D, flat shadow shapes only. Funny, never scary, for students aged 11 to 17.

Colors: use only the colors of the attached palette image (muted printing inks on cream paper): navy ink for the outlines, pale sky blue for the Czar's fur-trimmed coat (as on the attached character image), marigold yellow for its gold trim, deep crimson for the carriage, brown for the wheels and the fence, white and cream for the snow, cream parchment for the background.

Composition: horizontal 3:2 landscape format, the banquet window and the fence on the left, the limping carriage in the center, the Czar on the right, plain cream paper background around the scene. No text, no letters, no numbers, no signature.
```
