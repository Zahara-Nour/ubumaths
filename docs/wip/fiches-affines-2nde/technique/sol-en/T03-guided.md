To work out the image of a number under a function, we replace ~x~ with that number in the expression, putting negative numbers in brackets.

1.  a. ~f(0)=-3*0+4=4~: this is the y-intercept ~p~.
    b. ~f(2)=-3*2+4=-2~
    c. ~f(-1)=-3*(-1)+4=7~
    d. ~f(1/3)=-3*1/3+4=3~
    e. ~f(-2/3)=-3*(-2/3)+4=6~

2.  a. ~g(4)=4/2-1=1~
    b. ~g(-6)={-6}/2-1=-4~
    c. ~g(1)=1/2-1=-1/2~
