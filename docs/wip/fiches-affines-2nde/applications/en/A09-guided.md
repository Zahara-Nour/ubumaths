Let ~f~ be an affine function defined on $\mathbb{R}$ such that ~f(-1)=6~ and ~f(2)=-3~. Let $\mathcal{D}_f$ be the line representing it on a set of axes.

1. Finding the expression of ~f~.
   a. Work out the gradient ~m~ of ~f~, that is its rate of change between ~-1~ and ~2~.
   b. Deduce the y-intercept ~p~, then the expression of ~f(x)~.
2. Is ~f~ increasing or decreasing? Justify.
3. Sign of ~f(x)~.
   a. Solve the equation ~f(x)=0~.
   b. Draw the sign table of ~f(x)~ on $\mathbb{R}$.
4. The line $\mathcal{D}_f$.
   a. Draw $\mathcal{D}_f$ on a set of axes, by plotting the points of $\mathcal{D}_f$ with x-coordinates ~-1~ and ~2~.
   b. Explain how the line $\mathcal{D}_f$ shows whether ~f~ is increasing or decreasing, and then the sign of ~f(x)~.
