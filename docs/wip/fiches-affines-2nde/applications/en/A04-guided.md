A cinema offers two ways to pay:

- **option A**: €$9$ per ticket;
- **option B**: a membership card at €$36$ for the year, then €$5$ per ticket.

Let ~x~ be the number of tickets bought in the year, ~f(x)~ the total price paid with option A and ~g(x)~ the total price paid with option B, in euros.

1. Expressing the two options.
   a. Work out the total price paid for ~5~ tickets with each option.
   b. Express ~f(x)~ and ~g(x)~ in terms of ~x~.
   c. What kind of function is each of ~f~ and ~g~? Which one describes a proportional situation?
2. Comparing the two options.
   a. Solve the equation ~f(x)=g(x)~. Interpret the result and give the price paid in this case.
   b. Solve the inequality ~g(x)<f(x)~.
   c. From how many tickets is option B the better deal?
