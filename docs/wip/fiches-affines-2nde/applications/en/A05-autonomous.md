To hire a car for one day, two companies offer:

- **company A**: a fixed charge of €$50$, plus €$0.20$ per kilometre travelled;
- **company B**: a fixed charge of €$30$, plus €$0.30$ per kilometre travelled.

Let ~x~ be the distance travelled during the day, in kilometres, with $0\leqslant x\leqslant 400$, and let ~f(x)~ and ~g(x)~ be the prices paid, in euros, with company A and with company B. The lines $\mathcal{D}_f$ and $\mathcal{D}_g$ representing ~f~ and ~g~ are drawn below.

```courbe
x: 0 ; 400
y: 0 ; 160
grille: 50 ; 10
f(x) = 0.2*x+50 sur [0 ; 400]   bleu   nom=\mathcal{D}_f
g(x) = 0.3*x+30 sur [0 ; 400]   orange   nom=\mathcal{D}_g
axes: x = Distance (km) ; y = Price (€)
taille: grande
description: Two straight lines drawn from 0 to 400 km on a grid with steps of 50 km and 10 euros: the blue line D_f starts at 50 euros, the orange line D_g starts at 30 euros and rises faster; they cross at one point.
```

1. Express ~f(x)~ and ~g(x)~ in terms of ~x~.
2. Find graphically the distances for which company B is the cheaper one, then confirm it by calculation.
3. Which company should you choose for ~150~ km? For ~350~ km? Give the corresponding prices.
