A shop changes the prices of its items. Let ~x~ be the original price of an item, in euros.

1. An increase of $20\,\%$.
   a. An item costs €$50$. What is its price after an increase of $20\,\%$?
   b. Explain why, after an increase of $20\,\%$, the price is ~f(x)=1.2x~.
   c. What kind of function is ~f~? Give its multiplier.
2. Explain why, after a discount of $30\,\%$, the price is ~g(x)=0.7x~. What kind of function is ~g~?

3. Two changes in a row. The price of an item goes up by $20\,\%$, then the new price goes down by $30\,\%$.
   a. Show that the final price is ~h(x)=0.84x~.
   b. A customer thinks that the price went down by $10\,\%$ overall. Is the customer right? Give the overall percentage change.
4. After an increase of $20\,\%$, an item costs €$54$. What was its original price?
