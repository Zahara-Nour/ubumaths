A cinema offers two ways to pay:

- **option A**: €$9$ per ticket;
- **option B**: a membership card at €$36$ for the year, then €$5$ per ticket.

Let ~x~ be the number of tickets bought in the year, ~f(x)~ the total price paid with option A and ~g(x)~ the total price paid with option B, in euros.

1. Express ~f(x)~ and ~g(x)~ in terms of ~x~, then state what kind of function each one is.
2. For how many tickets do both options cost the same? What is this price?
3. From how many tickets is option B the better deal?
